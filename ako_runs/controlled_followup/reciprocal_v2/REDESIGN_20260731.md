# Reciprocal v2 corrective redesign

Status: **design-only and not launchable**. The source-frozen 48-cell manifests and
their evidence remain historical; this note and `redesign_v1.json` do not amend them.

The successor defaults to one honest translator and 24 cells
(`3 origins × 4 destinations × 2 modes`). The origin-by-destination interaction is
controlling only in the literal arm. Retuned results remain descriptive because the
historical retune grid does not identify origin.

A second translator may be added only when it is genuinely independent, isolated,
and receipt-backed; that choice requires a new 48-cell successor freeze. The default
one-translator design explicitly forfeits a translator-skill bound.

The enumerated ceiling is 24 translations, 240 audit candidate attempts, 480 screen
records, and 360 primary records: 1,080 execution records in total. Build failures
consume their attempt. No wall-clock or dollar estimate is invented before executors
exist.

`redesign_runner.py` now supplies CPU-only census/registry admission and an append-only
terminal-report journal. It deliberately does not execute translated sources: the
translation contract content-addresses `.py` bytes but defines no build, callable, audit,
or timing ABI. Choosing and freezing that material contract remains a launch blocker.
A successor also cannot launch until new manifests, implementations, the completed runner,
and pushed prelaunch provenance are frozen together. The historical `launch.py` remains
fail-closed and is not repurposed for the 24-cell successor.
