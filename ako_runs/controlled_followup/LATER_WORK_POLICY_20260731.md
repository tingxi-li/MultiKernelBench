# Later-work policy — 2026-07-31

## External validity and paper scope

No second-architecture performance campaign is authorized. Once a specific
second architecture is available, create a new hardware-bound successor and run
only the corrected 304-cell feasibility audit: source-retaining support probes,
build/setup/launch outcomes, and the frozen correctness gate. Do not run screen,
confirmation, sham, or timing stages there. The architecture-specific support
matrix and toolchain must be frozen and remotely verified before execution.

Draft one paper only after that matrix exists. Its controlling claims are
reachability across architectures and the settled-tail/sham resolution-floor
methodology for shrinking effect sizes. The sealed v1 timing ratios remain
scoped by their erratum and are not four-language epilogue evidence.

## RQ(e)

RQ(e), the distribution-sensitivity thesis, is dropped by default. Neither
crossed v1 nor v2 may revive it. Revival requires a separately funded and
preregistered public-corpus replication with paired `rand`/`randn` inputs, an
fp64 reference, corpus inclusion rules, candidate-independent gates, and a
declared inversion-rate estimand. That replication needs a new campaign ID,
budget, evidence chain, and paper scope; existing private Phase-1 observations
may motivate it but cannot control it.
